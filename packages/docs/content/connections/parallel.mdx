---
title: Parallel
description: Connect Parallel for agents to search the web and read web pages.
---

Agents search the web and read pages through Parallel, on the Parallel account you sign in with. What
they search for and the pages they read go to Parallel, and searches count toward that account's
usage. Nothing is kept in Personal Agent Proxy beyond the activity log.

## Connect Parallel

<Steps>

### Sign in with Parallel

Open **Connections**, then **Add connection**, and **Sign in with Parallel**. Sign in to your Parallel
account, or create one at [parallel.ai](https://parallel.ai), and approve access.

An organization has one Parallel connection: signing in again, with the same account or another,
gives it the new sign-in and keeps every agent's settings.

### Review the defaults

A new Parallel connection gives every agent Search the web and Read web pages. Turn either off on the
connection's page, or for one agent on the agent's page.

</Steps>

## What agents can do

- **Search the web** takes what to find, described in plain words, and answers with the pages that
  fit, each with its title, address, publish date where known and the passages that match.
- **Read web pages** opens one page by its address and answers with its title and text, the first
  100,000 characters of it.

Agents can't change anything through Parallel: both actions only read.
